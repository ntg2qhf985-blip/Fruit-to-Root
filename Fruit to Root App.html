Understanding Fruit to Root

What is Fruit to Root?

Fruit to Root is a gospel-centered reflection tool that helps you look beneath the surface of your behaviors, reactions, and emotions (the Fruit) to uncover the beliefs and heart attitudes driving them (the Root).

Instead of focusing only on changing what you do, you'll explore what you believe about God, yourself, and your circumstances—and how the truth of the gospel speaks to those beliefs.

Why do we do Fruit to Root?

  • •
    Our actions reveal what's happening in our hearts. Anger, worry, fear, and the desire to control aren't just isolated reactions. They can reveal what we're believing about God, ourselves, or our circumstances.
  • •
    Real change begins with the heart. We don't overcome sinful patterns simply by trying harder to behave better. We bring our thoughts and beliefs into the light, confess where we've believed lies, and turn back to God's truth.
  • •
    The gospel is the foundation for lasting change. Our hope isn't in our ability to fix ourselves, but in who God is and what He has done for us through Jesus Christ. As we grow in faith and learn to trust His truth, our lives begin to reflect the fruit of His Spirit.
